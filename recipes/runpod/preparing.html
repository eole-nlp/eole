Preparing your workspace

Connecting to your instance…

  1. Check persistent storage
  2. Check GPU
  3. Check prebuilt GPU kernels
  4. Download model
  5. Verify model files
  6. Load Qwen and prepare inference

GPU kernels arrive with the image; source compilation is a fallback. Qwen compiles its full MTP verifier locally on first startup; restarts reuse it from your volume. The workspace opens after preparation. Your rented GPU remains billable during preparation.
